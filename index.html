About me

I don’t sell more posts. I sell the right first sentence.

I’m Cyrille, a social media strategist working from Cameroon. I start where buyers start: with the problem they can already feel, in the words they already use. Then I connect it to the solution, the expertise and the proof.

My method has a name: the Problem-First Method. It came from auditing a real B2B company, ABCD Group, whose best social posts were exactly the ones closest to a customer’s daily pain.

What I won’t do

  • Monday-motivation filler
  • Post 16 times a day to “stay consistent”
  • Lead with jargon your buyer never uses

The Problem-First Method

1 · Problem the symptom they feel today
2 · Consequence what it costs them
3 · Solution introduced only now
4 · Expertise real people, visible
5 · Proof implementations, not claims
6 · Action one clear next step
The villain

The real enemy isn’t your content. It’s posting without positioning.

  • ✕ No clear answer to “what are you known for?”
  • ✕ Buyers meet your brand but can’t see their own problem in it
  • ✕ Likes pile up, leads don’t
  • ✕ Tried so far: post more, go viral, hire cheaper help. None fixes it.
