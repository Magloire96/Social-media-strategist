Strategy

The solution: Blue Ocean positioning × five psychology methods

Own “operational control for growth-stage African SMEs”, the moment a business outgrows Excel but doesn’t need a heavyweight consultancy. Guided by Joanna Wiebe’s five methods for influencing buyers, applied to ABCD:

1 Flip the statusExcel “heroics” become the embarrassing thing; control becomes the mark of a serious business.
→ Business Reality Check
2 Hijack the mythThe owner who sees everything. Attach to the story owners already tell about themselves.
→ The Growth Control Gap
3 Open the hidden doorTeach ERP and diagnostics in plain words, so buyers become “literate”.
→ Odoo Without Jargon
4 Build the ritualRecurring series on a fixed Tue/Thu/Sat rhythm people expect.
→ Office Wahala, Odoo in 60s
5 Give a superpowerShow what the owner can now do: see every branch without calling anyone.
→ From Excel to Control
